# Audit severity

- **Critical**: fabricated, corrupted, misidentified, or irreproducible evidence; privacy/license breach; conclusion may reverse.
- **Major**: unsupported central claim, material method omission, invalid comparison, missing uncertainty, or unaddressed contradictory evidence.
- **Minor**: traceability or reporting defect that does not presently change the interpretation.
- **Note**: improvement opportunity or question for expert judgment.

Automated validation can establish structural integrity, hashes, references, and required fields. It cannot establish that an experimental design is adequate, a physical model is appropriate, or a conclusion is scientifically true.
